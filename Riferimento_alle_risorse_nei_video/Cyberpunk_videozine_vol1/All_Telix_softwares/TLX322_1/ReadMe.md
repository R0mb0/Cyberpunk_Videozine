Recently there has been somewhat of a controversy surrounding the status of
copies of Telix purchased from various companies in the United Kingdom, and
how to order Telix from other sites in Europe.

This document shall be the final authority regarding the registration of
copies from the European continent, notably in the United Kingdom.

Exis, Inc. [hereinafter "Exis"], original developer of Telix from 1982
through late 1992, had contracted with ELSA GmbH of Aachen, Germany
[hereinafter "ELSA"], as the exclusive registration site for Europe.

ELSA and Exis then sold copies to a third registration site, Intershop, and
later to another company in Glasgow, Speedmodem, Ltd., both with a contact
person of George Collins [hereinafter "Intershop, et al"]. This arrangement
was for the convenience of users in the United Kingdom, enabling them to pay
in Sterling to a local site.  Intershop, et al, was only to contact ELSA, a
policy which was broken from time in order to expedite their demands.

Exis sold all rights to Telix to deltaComm Development, Inc. [hereinafter
"deltaComm"], on September 25, 1992. deltaComm and ELSA at that time renewed
the agreement for ELSA to be the exclusive distributor for Telix in Europe
(and recently renewed that agreement again in Germany, in January 1994).

The relationship between ELSA and Intershop, et al., soured slightly before
the change in copyright ownership for reasons which will not be discussed. At
that time, ELSA and deltaComm agreed that it would be in the best interests
of all involved to change registration sites in the United Kingdom. To that
end, ELSA has appointed as the new registration site for Telix:

                        S.C.S. Ltd.
                        Hall Road, Martham
                        Great Yarmouth
                        Norfolk, ENGLAND
                        NR29 4PD

It has also come to our attention that companies that George Collins either
controls or is associated with have continued to sell copies of Telix in
Great Britain without first purchasing them from ELSA or deltaComm. Such
copies may have had handwritten serial numbers on the disk labels that do not
match the serial number formats of a Telix serial number.  Some have been
labeled "provisional serial number" diskettes.

We regret to say that users who have purchased Telix in Great Britain who do
not have a serial number that 1) matches the serial number format of Telix,
and 2) cannot be accounted for as one of the several hundred that *were* sold
to Intershop, et al., may not in fact be registered Telix users. ELSA no
longer sells copies of Telix, directly or indirectly, to Intershop, et al.,
and has not since August, 1993.

The directors of Intershop, et al., have also refused requests to turn over
the complete database of the names and addresses of those who purchased Telix
from those companies. This will regrettably make it extremely difficult for
customers of Intershop, et al., to upgrade their copies via the new
distributor, S.C.S., Ltd.  We would like to try to reconstruct this database
as accurately as possible in order to make these upgrades possible, since
Intershop, et al., will not have access to official upgrades of Telix. We are
therefore sponsoring a disk swap to insure that all Intershop/Speedmodem
copies of Telix are removed from the market.

All users who purchased their copy or copies of Telix from Speedmodem or
Intershop should forward their name, address, original diskette and original
receipt dated prior to November 1st to deltaComm at the address at the top of
this document. We will replace each and every such copy with a legitimate
copy, one with a valid serial number. We at deltaComm will insure that S.C.S.
Ltd. is given each and every name and address we receive with a valid receipt
and disk, and that S.C.S. passes on to us the names and addresses of all new
registrees through them. The date mentioned is a date 30 days after the date
which we estimate, based on ordering patterns, that Intershop, et al., would
have certainly exhausted their supply of legitimate serial numbers purchased
from ELSA. Any copies purchased after that date, we regret we cannot replace.

All new copies of Telix in Great Britain should be purchased henceforth from
S.C.S., Ltd., at the address listed above. S.C.S., Ltd. may from time to time
sell to other dealers as they see fit. Any questions about the validity of a
dealer selling copies of Telix in the UK should be directed to S.C.S., Ltd.,
at the above address. A convenient order form has been enclosed in the latest
release of Telix to make this easier.  It is called UK.REG.

Please distribute this document as widely as possible within the United
Kingdom, so that we can insure that all copies sold henceforth will be sold
only through S.C.S., Ltd., the authorized registration site in the UK, or
through dealers to whom S.C.S., Ltd., may sell.

The current manner in which a European outside of the United Kingdom should
register Telix is to do so via ELSA's appointed agents.  Currently, only a
site in Germany has been appointed in addition to S.C.S., Ltd., in the UK.
Order forms for Europeans and their sites will be included with the latest
copy of Telix.  Version 3.22 includes EUROPE.REG and DEUTSCH.REG in addition
to UK.REG mentioned above.

Anything we can do to make your Telix experience more enjoyable we will
do.  Toward that purpose, ELSA will continue to seek local registration
sites for other European countries for inclusion in later versions of Telix.
Countries currently sought are The Netherlands, France, Spain, Finland,
Sweden and Switzerland.  Other countries may also be available.  To inquire
about the possibility of your company becoming an exclusive registration
site for your country in Europe, send a company profile and cover letter to:
