"Car.wld" is a learning experience for me.  I find that it is easier for me
learn by doing.  I like cars and work for one of the Big Three.  The vehicle
in the car world is not intended to represent any vehicle in style, size, 
etc..  There is a lot of additions that could be made to the world, some I
have on my to do list and is only a matter of time and some I am not sure
I can do without making additons to VR386.

Feel free to make changes and upload them for the rest of the VR world.  One
thing I would really like to do is to steer, accelerate, break, and so forth
from an external control center to the computer.  That is, an "gas pedal",
steering wheel, sound, appropriate movement of instruments and all.

Again, this world is a learning experience and very simplistic.  The more
complex objects were constructed using the Autocad to draw a polygon and then
using "dblist" to get the vertices.

I now have Northcad and this is suppose to create .PLG files directly.  I am
looking forward to learn this package and add to my world.

I am using a 33 MHz 486 clone, 8 Meg of memory, ATI Vantage with 2 Meg, a
Thompson multi-sync monitor, all running under MS-DOS 6.2.

Hope you enjoy this world and that it stimulates your imagination.  Comments
and suggestion are certainly welcomed.

Have a safe drive!!!!
