We've tried to reconstruct everything but it's not possible. Even if we
found all the files, we'd still be missing the readme's and some other
things. If you have the original disks from the magazines *please*
image them and upload them or let us know and we can help you copy them.
You will be credited, if desired. Same applies if you have any
of the missing files listed in any of the readme.txt files.

These are NOT the original disks - they are reconstructions.

Contents of disks 1-6 which were available by mail were mentioned at the
end of Issue 6. Please note these disks don't necessarily correlate with 
their respective issue number, they're related to articles throughout
different issues but kinda randomly organized, most likely to fit better on
the 5 1/4" 360k disks they used.

Under "Letters to the Editor" in issue 6 it mentions ""We chose
the 5.25" 360k floppy for compatibility purposes and cost.""
This made it a bit easier to verify if the contents were complete or not,
probably explains why the files are organized in this method as well.
Some disks are a little over but they were packed in different ways so
this makes sense.

It's still not clear why Dr. Dobbs X Sharp 15 was on it's own disk 
(mail order disk #5) when it only occupies ~64kb. According to issue 6
that's all that's on that disk. Given that the other disks were packed
full this doesn't seem correct but it's considered complete for now
until proven otherwise.
