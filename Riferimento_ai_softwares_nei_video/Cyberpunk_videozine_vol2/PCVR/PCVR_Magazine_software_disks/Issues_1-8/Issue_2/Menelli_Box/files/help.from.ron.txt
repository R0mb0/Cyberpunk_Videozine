>Is this an error on the schematic, or are there different pin-outs of
>this device available?  All the other pins seem the same, with 9..12
>being the TTL levels and 7/8/13/14 being the RS-232 level pins, it's
>just the actual functions that seem different.
>

Well, actually, I have the spec sheets from MAXIM, and they don't specify any
RS232 signal names in the pinout. This sort of makes sense, because all the
chip does is shift voltage levels irregardless of what signal is connected.
There are two RS232 to TTL converters and two TTL to RS232 converters in the
chip. It seems that your catalog simply chose the other pair of pins to connect
to TxD and RxD. Either method of connection should work.

>Also, the docs for your PG interface don't mention the cable used to
>connect the device to the serial port -- I presume you used a 
>null-modem cable?  In which case the standalone board should be
>wired up as if it's a DTE, yes?

No, actually the circuit is wired as a DCE device. Connect it as you would a
modem with a straight through cable.

>
>Finally (phew!) is there any *easy* way for me to tell whether my
>'HC11 is actually alive when I power it up?  Something like
>"connect to the port using kermit and type 'HELLO?'" would be
>very useful.  Makes you realise how much you take power-on self-test

Well, if everything is installed (program code and Power Glove included), the
power glove should beep, the lights on the PG sensor array should start 
flashing, and data should start appearing on whatever you have connected to the
serial port. Unfortunately, there are no other low level tests that I have 
installed.
